# Note on Technological Shelf Life

*This volume is the most exposed across the entire series to the passage of time: it incorporates market capitalization figures, regulatory analyses, IDE feature comparisons, and geopolitical assessments of recent events. This note declares what decays, what endures, and how to verify every factual claim.*

## 1. What Decays and What Endures

| Content Classification | Estimated Shelf Life | Verification Protocol |
|---|---|---|
| Model training costs, venture capital, and market sizes | Short (months) | Check annual vendor filings or cited agency reports; cross-check at least two primary sources. |
| Model leaderboards and benchmark comparisons | Very Short (weeks) | Consult current public leaderboards; never cite a benchmark from this volume without its date. |
| Legislation (EU AI Act, GDPR, PIPL, ethical frameworks) | Medium (1–3 years) | Consult the official consolidated legislative text, avoiding journalistic summaries. |
| Security frameworks (OWASP Top 10 for LLMs, NIST AI RMF) | Medium | Check the current active release published by the governing organization. |
| Architectural patterns (reactive, hybrid, graduated trust, structure over features) | Long (enduring) | Validated by first-principles reasoning and production telemetry, independent of transient tools. |
| Geopolitical analyses | Medium-Short | Review thoroughly before anchoring long-term capital allocation or vendor commitments. |

## 2. Two Operational Rules for the Reader

1. **Decouple the architectural argument from transient data points.** The core thesis of a chapter (for instance, that security trust is a continuous, graduated variable rather than a binary switch) survives even as the accompanying market valuation ages. When citing this volume in your own technical designs, cite the thesis and timestamp the data.
2. **Do not extrapolate individual case studies to entire sovereign states.** Geopolitical chapters describe concrete operating conditions within a specific regional ecosystem. What transfers to your architecture is the **underlying systemic variable to model** (vendor dependency, jurisdictional data sovereignty, access to advanced semiconductor nodes, talent density), never an uncritical generalization about an entire country.

## 3. Explicit Disclaimer on Industrial Case Studies and Geopolitics

The sections in Chapters 15 through 32 combine verifiable empirical facts (regulatory statutes, judicial rulings, institutional reports) with reasoned architectural projections (market consolidation trends, technical paradigm shifts). The manuscript explicitly cites primary sources where facts are verifiable. Where assertions are prospective, **treat them as authorial hypotheses, not established facts**: this is the exact epistemic discipline mandated in the Critical Reading Workbook.
