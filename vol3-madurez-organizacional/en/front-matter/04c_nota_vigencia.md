# Note on Technological and Regulatory Shelf Life

*This volume focuses on organizations, statutory regulations, and team practices. It is the least exposed across the entire series to raw technical obsolescence, yet simultaneously the most exposed to **regulatory** shifts: statutory frameworks evolve more slowly than foundation models, but with far greater systemic consequences.*

## 1. What Decays and What Endures

| Content Category | Operational Shelf Life | Verification Protocol |
|---|---|---|
| Regulatory Frameworks (AI Act, GDPR, liability directives) | Medium (1–3 years) | Consolidated official statutory text |
| Industry Metrics (DORA, enterprise productivity benchmarks) | Medium | Annual benchmark reports from issuing consortiums |
| Governance Frameworks (PMBOK, RACI matrices, OKRs) | Long | Current published standards versions |
| Organizational Practices (agentic sprints, agentic MVPs, ethics committees, katas) | Long | Validated by operational outcomes and organizational reproducibility, independent of specific tooling |
| Specific ROI and Cost Figures | Short | Recalculate using current pricing; the **methodology** outlined in each chapter outlives the figures |

## 2. What This Volume Explicitly Commits Not to Do

1. **It does not present organizational practices as universal dogmas.** Each chapter establishes its operational context (lean startups, highly regulated enterprises, QA-heavy engineering organizations) and states it explicitly. If your operating environment differs, apply the chapter as a decision rubric rather than an off-the-shelf prescription.
2. **It refuses to convert proxy metrics into targets.** This volume explicitly cautions against the trap of "perfect operational metrics on a failing project"; apply that warning rigorously when adopting the metric chapters themselves.

## 3. Maintaining the Vitality of This Volume

When adopting any template or rubric from this volume, document three metadata fields directly in your internal company artifact: (a) adoption timestamp, (b) the designated review owner, and (c) the explicit threshold conditions that render the document obsolete. This mirrors the exact revision hygiene the text demands for agent permissions and runtime execution policies—practicing it within your internal playbook proves that the foundational tenets of this book were mastered.
