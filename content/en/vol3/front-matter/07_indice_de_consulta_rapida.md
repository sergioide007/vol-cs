## 3. Cross-Volume Demarcation

If your challenge involves foundational systems architecture (agents, context windows, tools, orchestration patterns), consult Volume 1. If it involves cybersecurity attack surfaces, vertical industry case studies, or geopolitical dynamics, consult Volume 2. If it centers on runtime operations and scale (token economics, CIU metrics, chaos engineering, observability), consult Volume 4. This volume addresses **how to organize, govern, and upskill engineering teams** around agentic systems, not how to write their underlying code.

## 4. Operational Usage Warning

The templates and governance frameworks in this volume **are sensitive to organizational context**: the delivery cadence template in Chapter 6 presumes an engineering team empowered to refactor its own ceremonies, and the agentic MVP in Chapter 22 presumes a business sponsor willing to tolerate low-cost failure. Prior to implementing any framework, verify both preconditions; where either is absent, the respective chapter details a degraded, defensive fallback.
