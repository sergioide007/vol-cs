## 3. Demarcation Across the Series

If your objective is to **build from scratch** (agents, typed tools, context windows, orchestration patterns), consult Volume 1. If your focus is **runtime operations and scale** (token economics, CIU metrics, chaos testing, disaster recovery, observability), see Volume 4. If your mandate is **enterprise governance and culture** (roles, RACI, ethics committees, team topologies), consult Volume 3. This volume answers **where the technological frontier currently stands and where it is moving**, not how to assemble the base harness: for that, utilize the Reading Guide and Prerequisites Map.

## 4. Operational Usage Disclaimer

Chapters 15 through 32 contain time-sensitive market analyses and regulatory assessments. Prior to utilizing any market valuation or feature matrix in a high-stakes decision, apply the **Note on Technological Shelf Life**: timestamp the data point, verify against official primary filings, and maintain a rigorous boundary between empirical facts and authorial projections.
